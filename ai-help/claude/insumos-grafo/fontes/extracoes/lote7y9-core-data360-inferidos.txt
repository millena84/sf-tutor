Decisão: excluídos por C3 (Data 360) com "inferido": true, 20 tipos:
  CalculatedInsight, DataAction, DataActionTarget, DataGraph, DataLakeObject, DataModelObject, DataObjectSearchIndexConf, DataSource,
  DataSourceObject, DataSourceTenant, DataStreamBundle, DataTransform, ExternalDataConnector, ExternalDataSrcDescriptor,
  IdentityResolution, InternalDataConnector, MktDataConnectionParam, MktDataConnectionSrcParam, MktDataTranObject, DataConnectorIngestApi.
